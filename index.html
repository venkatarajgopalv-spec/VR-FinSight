About

Built to make ratio analysis understandable

VR FinSight was created to make financial ratio analysis easier to understand by connecting financial calculations with plain-language business interpretation — for founders, students, and analysts who know their numbers but want a clearer read on what those numbers mean together.

Every calculation is transparent: you can always see the exact formula and the exact figures used to reach a result.
