# VR FinSight

### See the numbers. Understand the business.

VR FinSight is an interactive financial analysis web application designed to transform financial data into meaningful financial insights.

Users can manually enter financial information, calculate key financial ratios, understand what each ratio means, identify potential financial concerns, and receive actionable recommendations.

---

- Formula explanation for every ratio

## 💡 How It Works

VR FinSight follows a simple financial analysis process:

**Enter → Calculate → Understand → Diagnose → Improve**

### 1. Enter

Users manually enter the financial information they have.

### 2. Calculate

The application calculates the applicable financial ratios.

### 3. Understand

Each ratio provides its formula, calculation, interpretation, and importance.

### 4. Diagnose

VR FinSight identifies potential financial strengths, weaknesses, and risks by looking at individual ratios and relationships between ratios.

### 5. Improve

The application provides practical recommendations based on the financial results.

---

## 🔎 Missing Information

Not every user will have every financial figure.

VR FinSight allows optional information to remain blank.

If a ratio cannot be calculated because required information is unavailable, the application clearly indicates that the data is unavailable rather than assuming a value of zero.

This helps maintain the accuracy and transparency of the analysis.

---

## 🧮 Ratio-wise Analysis

Each ratio provides:

- Calculated result
- Financial verdict
- Formula used
- User-specific calculation
- Explanation of what the ratio means
- Why the ratio matters
- Suggestions for improvement

Verdicts are presented using:

- 🟢 Healthy
- 🟡 Warning
- 🔴 Critical

General financial guidelines are used for interpretation, while recognizing that appropriate financial levels can vary across industries and businesses.

---

## 🧠 Financial Diagnosis

VR FinSight goes beyond calculating individual ratios.

It examines relationships between financial indicators to identify broader financial situations such as:

- Inventory-driven liquidity risk
- Receivables risk
- High financial leverage
- Weak interest coverage
- Profit margin pressure
- Working capital concerns

The final diagnosis summarizes the major strengths, weaknesses, and areas requiring attention.

---

## 📈 Financial Health Score

VR FinSight provides an overall Financial Health Score based on the available financial information.

The score provides a simplified view of:

- Liquidity
- Solvency
- Profitability
- Efficiency

The application also provides an explanation of the financial areas contributing to the overall assessment.

---

## 🎯 Priority Action Plan

The final analysis provides prioritized recommendations.

Examples include:

- Reducing excessive financial leverage
- Improving receivables collection
- Managing inventory efficiently
- Controlling operating expenses
- Improving profitability
- Strengthening working capital management

Recommendations are based on the financial information entered by the user.
